How we keep your clients separate

Client A never sees Client B. Not their people, not their documents, not their requests.

Every client company on Meridian HR is fully data-segregated — not just visually separated by a different logo, but isolated at the platform level. Access is scoped per client and per role: an employee at one company has no path, accidental or otherwise, to another company's information. It's the same guarantee you already make to your clients when you take on their HR — we just make sure the software keeps it too.

  • Isolated by design. Each client company's people, documents, and requests are architecturally separated, not just permission-flagged.
  • Role-based access, down to the individual. What an employee sees is scoped to their own company and their own role — never broader by default.
  • Compliance documentation available during your demo.
